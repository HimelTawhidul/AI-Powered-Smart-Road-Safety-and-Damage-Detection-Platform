# AI-Powered-Smart-Road-Safety-and-Damage-Detection-Platform

**Project Status: Planning / Implementation Not Started**

## About the Project

The AI-Powered Smart Road Safety and Damage Detection Platform is a proposed software solution designed to improve road safety and transportation management through Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, GPS technology, and real-time incident reporting.

The platform aims to detect road accidents, identify damaged roads and potholes, monitor flood-prone areas, and facilitate communication between citizens and relevant authorities.

## Project Objectives

- Improve road safety through intelligent accident detection.
- Detect potholes, cracks, and damaged road surfaces using AI.
- Monitor flood-prone and waterlogged roads.
- Provide real-time incident reporting and notifications.
- Support emergency response coordination.
- Help road authorities manage maintenance activities.

## Planned Features

1. **Accident Detection & Emergency Alerts**
   - AI-based accident detection.
   - Citizen accident reporting.
   - Emergency notifications.

2. **Road Damage Detection**
   - Pothole and crack detection.
   - Damage severity classification.
   - Geo-tagged damage reports.

3. **Flood Monitoring**
   - Flood-prone area mapping.
   - Citizen flood reporting.
   - Route hazard warnings.

4. **Citizen Portal**
   - User registration and login.
   - Accident and road damage reporting.
   - Report status tracking.

5. **Authority Dashboard**
   - Incident management.
   - Road repair task assignment.
   - Maintenance tracking and analytics.

6. **Emergency Response Dashboard**
   - Emergency alert management.
   - Ambulance and police notifications.
   - Response tracking.

## Target Users
